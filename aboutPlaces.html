Eiffel Tower

The Eiffel Tower is located in Paris, France. It was built by Gustave Eiffel's company and was completed in 1889. It was constructed for the Exposition Universelle, a world fair held in Paris. The Eiffel Tower is famous for its iron structure and beautiful design. It is one of the most recognizable landmarks in the world. Millions of tourists visit the Eiffel Tower every year.

colosseum

The Colosseum is located in Rome, Italy. It was built during the Roman Empire and was completed around 80 AD. It was used for public spectacles and entertainment in ancient Rome. The Colosseum is famous for its huge stone structure and historical importance. It is one of the most famous ancient monuments in the world. Today, it is an important tourist attraction in Rome.

Statue of Liberty

The Statue of Liberty is located in New York City, USA. It was a gift from France to the United States and was dedicated in 1886. The statue represents freedom and liberty. It is famous for its large copper statue and torch held high in one hand. The Statue of Liberty is one of the most famous symbols of the United States. Millions of people visit it every year.

Great Wall of China

The Great Wall of China is located in China. It is a huge series of walls and fortifications built over many centuries. Different parts of the wall were built and expanded by different Chinese dynasties. It was mainly constructed for defense and protection. The Great Wall is famous for its enormous length, ancient history and beautiful views. It is one of the most famous landmarks in the world.

Burj Khalifa

The Burj Khalifa is located in Dubai, United Arab Emirates. It was officially opened in 2010. It is the world's tallest building, reaching a height of 828 metres. The building has a modern and unique design. It contains offices, residences, hotels and observation areas. Burj Khalifa is one of the most famous modern landmarks in the world.

Sydney Opera House

The Sydney Opera House is located in Sydney, Australia. It was officially opened in 1973. It is famous for its unique roof design, which looks like sails. The building is used for concerts, theatre performances, opera and other cultural events. It is located near Sydney Harbour and is one of Australia's most recognizable landmarks. It is also a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Pyramids of Giza

The Pyramids of Giza are located near Cairo, Egypt. They were built thousands of years ago during ancient Egypt. The pyramids were built as tombs for pharaohs and are among the oldest and most famous monuments in the world. The Great Pyramid of Giza is the largest of the three main pyramids. The pyramids are famous for their enormous size, ancient history and engineering.

Christ the Redeemer

Christ the Redeemer is located in Rio de Janeiro, Brazil. It is a large statue of Jesus Christ standing on the top of Mount Corcovado. The statue was completed in 1931. It is famous for its huge size, open-arm design and beautiful view of Rio de Janeiro. It is one of Brazil's most famous landmarks and an important tourist attraction.

Petra

Petra is an ancient city located in Jordan. It was an important city of the Nabataean civilization. Petra is famous for its buildings and tombs carved directly into the rose-coloured rock. One of its most famous structures is the Treasury. The city has a long and interesting history and is one of the world's most famous archaeological sites. Petra is also a UNESCO World Heritage Site.
